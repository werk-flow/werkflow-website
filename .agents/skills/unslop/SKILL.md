# Unslop

Apply to every piece of prose. Preserve the meaning, facts, and material conditions.

Lead with the point. Use plain verbs, concrete nouns, and active voice. Split sentences that carry several thoughts. Vary sentence length without making the reader work.

Remove flattery, promotional filler, vague attributions, invented metaphors, decorative labels, and stock conclusions. Cut words such as "leverage" when "use" says the same thing. State the positive point directly rather than forcing an "X, not Y" contrast.

Use sentence-case headings when they help navigation. Avoid decorative emojis, em dashes, and excessive bold text. The established document Status separator is exempt. Preserve exact quotations and upstream license text.

Read the result once for meaning and once for needless words. Keep necessary uncertainty. Do not replace a precise qualification with a stronger unsupported claim.

For customer-facing copy, also apply the copywriting skill and its fifth-grade reading-level requirement. This style pass alone does not establish reading level.
