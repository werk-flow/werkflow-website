# Technical writing

Read the repository's doc index and the owning source before editing. Choose whether the document explains a concept, records reference facts, or guides a task. Keep independently useful topics in their owning documents.

Use English for internal docs. State confirmed decisions, observations, hypotheses, and open choices distinctly. Name who owns a decision and what evidence supports a factual claim.

Keep changing facts in one home. Link to sibling owners instead of copying their policies. Use relative links that resolve from the document's actual location, and check heading fragments. Describe missing sources explicitly.

Update the status date after substantive review and maintain the local index. A date is not proof that the content agrees with the product. For availability, verify the relevant release rather than relying on planned scope or a dirty checkout.

Use direct sentences and ordered steps for procedures. Link detailed evidence where readers need it. Apply unslop and finish by removing unnecessary sections and repeated facts. Use the repository workflow's review checks.
